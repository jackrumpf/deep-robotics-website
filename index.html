Autonomous ground infrastructure for unmanned fleets

We take people out of the ground loop: recovering, servicing and relaunching uncrewed aircraft at forward and remote sites.

Request a briefing What we do

Unmanned fleets still run on human logistics

A small uncrewed aircraft can fly for under an hour. Then someone on the ground has to recover it, check it over, restore its power and send it back out. At a forward or remote site, that person is the scarce resource, and every additional aircraft adds to the load.

  • Every additional vehicle adds to the ground-support burden.
  • Manual servicing limits turnaround and persistent operations.
  • Different platforms need different ground equipment and procedures.

  1. Recover

    Detect and receive returning aircraft without an operator on site.

  2. Service

    Inspect each aircraft and restore it at the point of landing.

  3. Relaunch

    Verify it is ready, clear the pad and send it back to the mission.

Infrastructure, not airframes

Deep Robotics builds the ground layer that keeps drone operations persistent. We don't build aircraft. We build the systems that receive them, look after them and get them back in the air, so that fleets can grow without the ground crew growing with them.

Unattended

A full turnaround with nobody at the landing point, so operators stay with the mission instead of the aircraft.

Platform-agnostic

Built to service more than one airframe from the same system, rather than tying a site to a single aircraft.

Field-ready

Sized for forward and remote sites, set up on unprepared ground and run from standard power.

Safe by design

The pad is monitored for debris and faults, and the system shuts down safely the moment something is wrong.

Engineers from aerospace and defense

Our founding team brings hands-on experience from commercial spaceflight, the defense industry and the U.S. Air Force, and the depth to take flight hardware from design through build and test.
