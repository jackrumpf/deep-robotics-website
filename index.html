Unmanned fleets still run on human logistics

Every uncrewed aircraft in the field depends on a crew on the ground to recover it, check it, recharge it and send it back out. As fleets grow, that burden grows with them.

Deep Robotics builds the autonomous infrastructure that closes that loop. Our systems handle the turnaround at the point of landing, reduce the people needed at forward positions, and are designed to work across platforms rather than for a single airframe.

Our first system is in development. Briefings are available to government and industry partners on request.

  • Recover

    Receive returning aircraft without an operator on site.

  • Service

    Inspect and restore each aircraft at the point of landing.

  • Relaunch

    Clear it for the next mission and send it back out.
